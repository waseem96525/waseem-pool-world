❓ How to Play

🎯 Shooting

  • Drag backward from the cue ball and release to shoot.
  • Longer drag = more power. Watch the power bar.
  • Use the Spin slider to apply topspin or backspin.

🎮 Controls

  • Undo reverses your last shot.
  • New starts a fresh game.
  • Theme cycles table colors.

🖐️ Ball in Hand

  • After a foul, the cue ball is "in hand."
  • Tap anywhere on the table to place it, then shoot.

🏆 Game Modes

  • 8-Ball: Pocket all your group, then the 8-ball.
  • 9-Ball: Pocket the 9-ball to win.
  • Straight: Pocket as many balls as possible.

💡 Tips

  • Build combos by pocketing multiple balls per turn.
  • The timer keeps the game moving — don't stall!
  • Topspin rolls forward; backspin pulls back.
