📜 How to Play

🎯 Shooting

  • Drag backward from the cue ball to aim.
  • Drag further = more power (up to 100%).
  • Release to fire.

🎯 Aim Assist

  • White dashed line = cue ball trajectory
  • Ghost ball = where cue ball will contact
  • Green line = predicted object ball path
  • Orange line = cue ball deflection
  • Gold ring = pocket the object ball is aimed at
  • Angle display = shot direction in degrees

💰 Earning Coins

  • +5 coins per ball pocketed
  • +10 bonus per combo ball
  • +50 to +150 for winning
  • +500 to +5000 for tournaments
